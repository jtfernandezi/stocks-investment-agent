### Lever 5 — Guardrails 🔴 (the core finding)

**The mechanical trailing-stop layer silently failed on the 06-22 morning batch.** Forensics from Alpaca `/orders`:
- 06-22 **13:32:20 UTC (09:32 ET)** — 6 market orders submitted at once (C, CEG, GD-sell, HBM, RTX-sell, VRT). **Zero trailing-stop orders were submitted for any of them.**
- The midday batches (CCJ+OKLO at 15:42, AMAT+DLR+ZS at 16:02) **did** get trailing stops.
- **Root cause = fill latency vs `Wait For Fill`.** VRT's buy filled at **13:38:48 — 6m28s after submission**; C similar. The n8n `Wait For Fill` node waits **5 seconds** before `Needs Trailing Stop? → Submit Trailing Stop`. When the batch of 6 simultaneous market orders fills slowly, the trailing-stop (sell) order fires while the long buy is still open → Alpaca rejects it ("cannot open a short sell while a long buy order is open" — the exact failure mode the 5s wait was added for on 2026-05-28, just under-provisioned for slow batch fills). The midday orders filled in seconds, so their stops succeeded.
- **Live blast radius: real.** CEG and HBM (also unprotected) were later closed by the orchestrator/reconciliation; **C (−2.2%) and VRT (−11.0%) are still open with no stop at all.** VRT has round-tripped to −11% with nothing protecting it — the precise "never protected" failure Lever 5 exists to catch.
- **Why nobody noticed:** the canary reconciles positions vs DB but does **not** verify each position has a protective stop. Detection is this week's `[B-code]` PR; prevention (lengthen `Wait For Fill`, or — more robustly — a Watchdog "ensure every position has a trailing stop" reconciliation that submits a missing one) is n8n wiring → `[B-spec]`.
- Historical: `thesis_flip` remains the worst exit category; `trailing_stop` exits this week were orderly on the *protected* names.
