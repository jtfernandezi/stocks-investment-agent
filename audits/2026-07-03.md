### Lever 4 — Orchestrator judgment 🔴 (the core finding)
- **The TREND-veto deadlock.** Every session 06-29→07-02 tells the same story, near-verbatim: *"All new specialist ideas across defense, semis, SaaS, nuclear, data centers, healthcare, and financials are framed as TREND entries, yet our feedback engine [shows TREND negative-EV]"* → zero actions. On 07-01 it closed BAC (−$14) explicitly *"to align with the feedback system's clear conclusion that TREND-pattern trades are currently negative EV."* The book went 5 positions → 2.
- Mechanism verified in code and data: `formatPatternPerf` renders `TREND: win 24% … EV -1.50% (17 trades) ← NEGATIVE EV — DO NOT TRADE` (the n=17 sample legitimately clears the ≥5 floor), and §7b rule 1 says a negative-EV pattern with ≥5 trades blocks new positions *"regardless of signal quality."* Because TREND is the catch-all label (68% of all closed trades; every entry since 06-08 but one), this is arithmetic equivalent to "the fund has lost money overall → never trade again." A pattern that covers ~everything discriminates ~nothing.
- Fairness note: the orchestrator is *obeying its instructions*, and its caution has a real basis (24% win rate when it deploys). The defect is in the rule we gave it, not in its compliance. But a fund at 98% cash cannot beat SPY by construction and cannot generate the closed trades that would ever revise the EV — the definition of a self-sealing loop. **PR #18** replaces the categorical veto with a dominance-aware annotation (judge setups individually; express caution via sizing).
- Divergence worth watching: specialists 80% bullish, orchestrator watchlist 100% bearish (CRWD, FTNT, GD, LAC, PFE, SMR). If PR #18 unblocks entries, watch whether it deploys short-only against a bullish specialist book.

### Lever 7 — System improvement
The through-line of five audits is now unmistakable: **every brake in the system eventually routes to the same failure — chronic non-deployment** (cold-start caps → 06-05; EV on tiny samples → 06-12/17; qualitative TREND-aversion → 06-21; and now sanctioned ≥5-sample pattern-EV → 07-03). Each fix closed one channel and the behavior re-emerged through the next legitimate one. PR #18 closes the last *mechanical* channel with a discrimination test (dominance ≥60% → EV is not pattern information). If the behavior re-routes *again* — through pure prose this time — that will be strong evidence the fix must be architectural (a positive deployment mandate tied to a quantified regime input), not annotational. That is the macro-regime governor, and it still needs the backtest harness to be verifiable. The harness remains the keystone and the top unfunded item.

The deeper structural insight this week: the pattern taxonomy itself is broken as a feedback dimension. When one label absorbs 68% of trades, pattern-EV is a constant, not a variable. Either the classifier needs finer grain (TREND-early vs TREND-extended by distance from the 20d MA; pullback-within-trend vs breakout) or the feedback should pivot to dimensions with real variance (niche, side, regime-at-entry). Logged as a new `[B-spec]` initiative.
