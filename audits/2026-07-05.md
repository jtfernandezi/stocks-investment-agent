### Lever 7 — System improvement 🟢 (the week's real work)

**7a-1. The veto-resurrection fragility (found, fixed — PR #21).** Chain of facts: (i) post-Phase-1, every new trade stores a price-based label, so legacy rows can never gain a sample — TREND is frozen at 15 trades / EV −1.70% forever; (ii) the orchestrator still *proposes* in legacy terms (signal-persistence TREND/BIAS is what §6a/§7 sizing rules speak); (iii) the dominance guard reads `share >= 0.6` and TREND sits at exactly 15/25 = 60.0%; (iv) OXY — one of only two open positions — is labeled BIAS, so its close alone drops TREND to 15/26 = 57.7% → flag flips back to `← NEGATIVE EV — DO NOT TRADE` → the fund-wide veto returns, **permanently**, because the frozen row can't update. During the 06-24→07-02 deadlock the orchestrator quoted this table flag verbatim every session — the flag, not the prose, is the operative signal. PR #21 renders legacy rows as `LEGACY LABEL — historical context only, NOT an entry gate`, scopes the veto/dominance/LOW-EV flags to active price-based rows, and computes dominance within the active record (so a young catch-all price label can't escape the guard via a frozen denominator). Phase 1's §7b prose already declared legacy rows "history, not guidance" — this makes the rendering agree.

**7a-2. Read honestly: **the short side of Phase 1's extension gate is evidence-backed; the long side is contradicted-in-period** — the extension curve is U-shaped and the gate confines the fund to the weakest middle (±2% ≈ +2.4% avg), while the momentum tail it blocks was the period's best long profile *except in BEAR regimes*. This does NOT mean "revert the gate": the period is one momentum-heavy cycle, buy-and-hold-21d ≠ a stopped position, and the fund's actual 06-22 disaster was batch-clustering at a local top (the throttle's domain). It means the long gate should probably become **regime-conditioned** (block extended longs in BEAR/NEUTRAL, allow with reduced size in computed BULL) — a judgment change to a 2-day-old operator-built control, so it is raised as `[B-spec]`, not coded. The regime-contrarian finding (BEAR → best forward returns) similarly cautions against hard-wiring the exposure bands tighter until more sample exists.
